## Present dramatically. Operate calmly. Scale completely.

Orbital Archive No. 01 combines archival aerospace drafting, Swiss-modernist clarity, restrained cinematic depth, and pragmatic product UX. It creates atmosphere through geometry, typography, controlled light, and tactile surfaces—never through fake telemetry or decorative complexity.

Open **[the complete visual guide](index.html)** locally in any browser. No install, server, package manager, or build step is required.

## The depth model

| Depth | Mode | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0 · Signal** | Cinematic editorial | Orient, establish identity, invite one next action |
| **1 · Mission** | Calm operational | Support active work with restrained hierarchy |
| **2 · Systems** | Deliberately technical | Reveal diagnostics, advanced settings, logs, and dense detail |

Every deeper context keeps its scope visible and provides a clear route back.

## Aerospace without costume

The identity is architectural: orbital arcs, horizon curves, plotted trajectories, datum crosshairs, leader lines, measurement ticks, calibration rails, coordinate notation, blueprint construction geometry, and quiet registration marks.

Paper grain, film noise, scanlines, and grid texture may give surfaces material character at restrained opacity. They never sit between the user and the task.

Ignition Orange is rare and editorial. Relay Blue remains the operational action color. Status colors retain explicit meanings.

## Use it anywhere

The package is intentionally framework-free. Use the HTML, CSS, SVG, JSON, and token exports directly or translate the semantic patterns into React, Vue, Svelte, Angular, native mobile, desktop, game UI, embedded systems, presentations, or server-rendered projects.

## Non-negotiables

- Orient first, expose complexity deliberately, and preserve a return path.
- The first viewport answers: **Where am I? What state is this in? What can I do next?**
- Use schematic detail to express real alignment, scale, sequence, or relationship.
- Keep operational interfaces calmer than editorial entry points.
- Do not invent niche pictograms. Use a clear label or a vetted library symbol.
- Review icon geometry at 16, 20, 24, 32, and 48 pixels.
- Never encode state by color alone.
