Guide 04 · Composition

Build from signal to systems.

Every page begins by answering where the user is, what state the system is in, and what they can do next.

Core rule

Let the primary task carry the visual weight. Technical atmosphere belongs behind information, never on top of it.

Information remains primary over construction detail.

Choose a depth

Use Signal for orientation, Mission for active work, and Systems only for deliberately entered advanced detail.

Preserve the sequence

On narrow screens, keep context first, place the primary work next, and move supporting material after it.
